    What this is

    back to the top ↑

    A trading bot that runs itself, with money that isn't real.

    Every four hours a language model wakes up, looks at the price of Ethereum, and decides whether to buy, sell, or do nothing. Before it is allowed to act it has to write down what it expects to happen and by when, in plain words. Then it goes back to sleep. Everything further up this page is what it has actually done.

    It started with $1,000 of pretend dollars. Nothing is really bought or sold, but the prices are real and the trades are charged real fees, so the number at the top is roughly what would have happened. It can only buy and hold ETH, with no borrowing, no shorting and no leverage. A stop price is enforced in code rather than by the model, so a bad decision has a floor under it.

    The blue line is the whole point. It is what the same $1,000 would have done if someone had bought ETH on day one and then gone outside. Beating a chart anyone can follow is the bar, and there is no particular reason to expect this thing clears it. Finding out is what it's for.

    Nothing here is advice, and nobody is grading the bot's predictions yet. That comes once there are enough of them to be worth reading.

    wake
    One check of the market, every four hours. Most of them it skips without thinking hard, because nothing moved enough to be worth it.
    the stop
    A price at which the position is sold automatically, with no decision involved. Checked every minute, whatever the model thinks.
    round trip
    A buy and the sell that eventually closes it. Only then is a profit or loss real rather than on paper.
    paper money
    Simulated. Real prices, real fees, imaginary dollars.

    Paper money. Nothing here is advice, and the bot is not good at this yet.
